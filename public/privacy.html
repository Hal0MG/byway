Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Byway runs on your own computer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The people who make the app don't receive your trips, your keys or anything you type. To plan and map a trip, the app does contact some outside services, and this page says which ones and what they get.

What stays on your computer

  • Your trips, with their notes, versions and questions, in the app's data folder.
  • Your keys and contact (the ones you enter in Setup), in the app's .env file.
  • Caches of routes, places, weather, photos and searches, in the data folder, so the same lookups aren't repeated.
  • Preferences in your browser's local storage: theme, last view, remembered trip options, map layers.

Deleting a trip removes it from the trips file. Deleting the app folder removes everything above.

On the public demo

On the demo website (not on a copy running on your own computer), the app's keys belong to the site's owner, and you never enter one. A random id in a cookie tells the server which trips are yours, so you only see your own, and it counts how much planning each visitor has used in a day. Your saved trips are kept on the demo's server and, as a backup, in your browser's storage, because the free host clears the server's disk when it sleeps. The demo's trips and counts aren't used for anything else. Clear the site's data in your browser to remove your copy; trips on the server disappear when the host next clears it.

Your location

The Today screen can use your device's location to show what's next. It's used in your browser only, isn't saved and isn't sent to the app's server. If you open a day in Google Maps or Apple Maps from Today, your current position is the starting point in that link.

Sharing

A read-only link from "Export and share" lets anyone on the same network who has the link view that trip while the app is running. Exported files (GPX, PDF, the trip page) go wherever you send them.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything about anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version comes with the app and the date at the top changes with it.
